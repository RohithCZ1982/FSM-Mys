## 🎥 Filming Tips for the School (DIY)

If you are filming this yourself with a phone or camera:
*   **Lighting**: Always film with the window *behind* you. Let natural light hit the children's faces.
*   **Angles**: Get down to the *child's eye level*. Don't film from above; kneel down to see the world as they do.
*   **Stability**: Use a tripod or hold your elbows tight to your body to avoid shaky footage.
*   **Resolution**: Shoot in **4K at 60fps** (frames per second) if possible, so you can create smooth slow-motion shots later.
